title: "Regime study: gold in the 2020 pandemic shock"
tags: [regime, crisis, macro, rates, india]
---

2020 compressed a full gold cycle into a single year. March 2020 replayed 2008 in
miniature: in the dash-for-cash, gold fell about 12% in two weeks as leveraged books
liquidated. Then the policy response — rates cut to zero, unprecedented fiscal
transfers, real 10-year US yields falling to roughly −1% — produced the fastest
gold bull leg in a decade, with USD spot printing an all-time high above $2,060/oz
in August 2020.

The real-yield mechanism was unusually clean: from March to August 2020, the fall in
10-year TIPS yields tracked the rise in gold almost point-for-point. When the real
return on the safest asset is negative one percent, an asset that merely holds its
purchasing power dominates it.

India-specific texture: with jewellery shops closed through lockdowns, physical demand
collapsed and the domestic price traded at a rare DISCOUNT to landed cost, even as the
INR price hit records (the rupee had weakened to ~₹75/USD). Households were net
sellers of old gold — recycling supply surged. Investment demand migrated to paper
routes: Sovereign Gold Bond tranches saw record subscriptions.

The 2020 lesson set: the crisis playbook is liquidation-then-QE-bid; real yields are
the transmission channel; local premium/discount reveals the physical market's state
independent of the headline price; and paper routes (SGB) matter when buying is
motivated by investment rather than adornment.
